# Milestone 04: site, SEO and content (tegelapps.com) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A static marketing and SEO site for Tegel on `tegelapps.com` (Cloudflare Pages), built by a ~100-line bun script that reuses the app's own `renderToHTML`. It runs no script of our own outside `/tools/*` (plan 08). It goes live in W2 with GSC verified and the imprint (business name, address, KvK number) in place, adds legal pages in W5 and the download page in W6, and publishes two articles a week from W8.

**Architecture:**
- `site/build.js` reads `site/content/<lang>/**/*.md` and parses front matter with `Bun.YAML.parse`. It renders the body with `Renderer/src/markdown.js` `renderToHTML` into `site/templates/layout.html`, then writes `site/public` with sitemap, robots, 404 and RSS.
- Prices, URLs and legal ids come from one file, `site/config.json`. Content refers to them through `{{placeholders}}`.
- bun tests assert SEO and honesty invariants over the built output.
- Deploys use `wrangler pages deploy`, run from `site/` so plan 08's `site/functions/` uploads with `public/` (X9).
- Scripts: Cloudflare's analytics beacon, which Pages injects at deploy time and the privacy page discloses, and, only on `/tools/*`, the tool's own same-origin `main.js` (plan 08). One form: the waitlist (plan 08), a plain POST to the same-origin Pages Function `/api/waitlist`.
- Design values come only from `DESIGN.md` and plan 09's `site/static/css/tokens.css`.

**Tech stack:**
- bun 1.4.0 (`~/.local/bin/bun`): `Bun.YAML`, `Bun.Glob`, `HTMLRewriter` and `bun:test`, all checked locally on 27 Oct.
- `renderToHTML` from plan 01 (markdown-it and highlight.js are already in `Renderer/`).
- Cloudflare Registrar, DNS, Email Routing, Pages and Web Analytics [F8].
- `bunx wrangler`, `bunx lighthouse` (Node 22.23 and Google Chrome are installed on the build Mac).
- Google Search Console and Google Trends, both through the user's browser or the Playwright MCP.
- gstack browse (`B=~/.claude/skills/gstack/browse/dist/browse`) for visual QA, the OG render and `/design-review` (CLAUDE.md Tools).

**Spec:**

- `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-00-master-roadmap.md`: D7, D8, D9, D15, §3.1, §3.2, H3, H4, H11, §6 and §7.

## Global Constraints

- **Product rule 3:** "Text, code and diagrams only. The icon is a plain geometric tile. No faces, figures, animals, mascots or Delft figurative tiles. Arabic sample text is never Qur'an or hadith." This covers screenshots, `og.png`, the press kit and article images.
- **Product rule 4:** "No music or audio anywhere (apps, demos, site)."
- **Product rule 5:** "No holiday theming (the December launch included). No giveaways by draw, no 'spin to win'."
- **Product rule 6:** "A dated introductory price only, never a fake 'was' price (art. 5a Bpp). No fake counters or scarcity. Licence limits are stated before purchase. 14-day refund."
- **Product rule 8:** "No ads, in or around the apps."
- **Launch-price wording, exact (BUSINESS-PLAN §8):** "Introductory price $14 until Mon 14 Dec 2026 23:59 CET. From 15 Dec: $19." The price really rises on that date, and the site never shows a "was" price.
- **Prices (master Global constraints):** App 1 "$19 once, 2 Macs", introductory "$14 until Mon 14 Dec 2026 23:59 CET"; App 2 "$15". The bundle ("$29", one licence key per app) belongs to plan 06 (W14, P1-9). It goes on the site at `/bundle/` only once its Polar product exists.
- **Free tier, App 1:** "Quick Look preview of `.md`; single-file window; syntax highlighting; Mermaid; RTL."
- **Paid tier, App 1:** "folder mode (sidebar, cross-document search, live reload); paginated A4/Letter PDF export that is RTL-correct; 3 typographic themes with light/dark."
- **Licence:** perpetual for 1.x, 2 Macs (2 activations [F3e]), 14-day no-questions refund. Polar is the merchant of record (MoR).
- **D9:** no static-site-generator dependency and no new Markdown dependency. The site reuses `renderToHTML`.
- **Forms and scripts (A5):** one form: the waitlist (plan 08). Scripts only on `/tools/*` (the tool's own same-origin `main.js`, plan 08) and the injected Cloudflare beacon (decision A1), which the CSP allows by name and `/privacy/` discloses. No third-party script.
- **Design (P1-3/P1-5):** every UI or site-design task starts by reading `DESIGN.md`; no value outside it. System fonts only, one served stylesheet (`/site.css` = plan 09's `tokens.css` + `site.css`), light and dark.
- **Legal ids:** the KvK registration already exists. `KVK_NUMBER` in `.env` fills `site/config.json` `kvk`; the user's answer (U-6) fills the registered business name and the address. The imprint carries all three from the first deploy, and the build fails while any is empty.
- **Approval first (CLAUDE.md):** purchases, the first public deploy and DNS changes on a live domain. Approval covers the specific action shown, not the next one; preview deploys need none.
- **Secrets:** keep them in `.env` (chmod 600) and refer to them by key name only. Never `echo` a value or put one in a commit, a plan or a log.
- **Evidence:** claim "works on macOS 26/27", "on Apple Silicon" or any feature only when plan 05's test matrix or 1.0.0 build shows it.
- **Build Mac:** Intel, macOS 15.7, bun at `~/.local/bin/bun`, Node 22.23, Google Chrome in `/Applications`.

## Review Focus

Each line gives the input, the expected behaviour and the task whose tests pin it.

1. **Price drift after 15 Dec** (Polar charges $19 while a page, its JSON-LD or a translation still says $14, or the reverse) → every price comes from `site/config.json`; on 15 Dec the site switches first, then Polar, then a fresh checkout is checked (X15). *Task 4 (source scan, JSON-LD price) and Task 15 (`curl` on production).*
2. **A page added or renamed** → the alternates stay reciprocal, every canonical is the page's own absolute URL, and a published slug never changes. *Task 4 tests; slug rule in Task 16.*
3. **Mixed bidi on the localised pages** (Arabic with English terms, `$14` and code) → `<html dir="rtl">`, with every `<pre>` kept `dir="ltr"`. *Task 4 test, run on the fixture and the real site.*
4. **Pages arriving in phases** (for example, a nav link to `/download/` before W6) → no internal link or asset 404s in any phase. *Task 5 "every internal link and asset resolves".*
5. **Preview URLs indexed as duplicates** of `tegelapps.com` → every `*.pages.dev` response sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`. *Task 5 `_headers` test, plus a `curl -sI` on the preview in Task 8.*

## Decisions taken in this plan

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics**, enabled with the Pages one-click setting (Metrics → Web Analytics), disclosed on `/privacy/`. No cookie banner | BUSINESS-PLAN §4 tests "conversion from visit to sale ≥ 1%", which needs a visit count. GSC counts only Google clicks, so it misses launch traffic from PH, HN and Reddit. Cloudflare says Web Analytics "does not use any client-side state, such as cookies or localStorage" and does not fingerprint [S3]. Low-impact analytics need no consent, only disclosure (ACM, AP) [S15]. The repo ships no analytics script; Pages adds the beacon "on the next deployment" [S2]. Rejected "none": it leaves the §4 test unmeasurable |
| A2 | URLs: English at the root, other languages under `/<lang>/`, always with a trailing slash. **A published slug never changes**; a forced move adds a line to `site/static/_redirects` | Pages redirects `/x/index.html` to `/x/` [S6]; renamed URLs lose GSC history |

| A4 | The site chrome (nav and footer) stays in English on the localised pages | Ponytail: 3 pages. Add per-language UI strings if GSC shows NL/FR/AR traffic |

## File structure and shared interfaces

```
site/
├─ build.js                 # build(), fill(), loadConfig(): ≤ ~110 lines (D9)
├─ config.json              # SINGLE source of prices, URLs, legal ids        [new vs §3.1]
├─ lighthouse.sh            # serve site/public locally, audit every sitemap URL [new vs §3.1]
├─ content/<lang>/**/*.md   # en at the root URL; nl, fr, ar under /<lang>/
├─ templates/layout.html    # one layout; tokens below
├─ templates/partials/      # plan 08: waitlist-form.html, inserted by {{partial:waitlist-form}}
├─ static/                  # copied to public/ (except css/): site.css, css/tokens.css (plan 09), favicon.svg, og.png, _headers, img/, media/, press/
├─ functions/api/           # plan 08: waitlist.js Pages Function (uploaded because deploys run from site/)
├─ tools/<name>/            # plan 08: main.js (bundled to public/tools/<name>/), tool.html
└─ test/                    # scan.js (helper), site.test.js (invariants), pages.test.js (phase lists), fixtures/, tools/demo/  [new vs §3.1]
```

**Interfaces:** everything in this block is new; the §3 names are used verbatim.

- **Front matter** (YAML, parsed with `Bun.YAML.parse` and stripped before rendering):
  - `title` (≤ 60 characters) and `description` (70–160 characters);
  - `slug` (`''` = home, `'404'` = the 404 page, `'articles/<x>'` = an article);
  - `lang` (`en|nl|fr|ar`, which must equal the folder name);
  - `alternates` (map from lang to path, optional) and `updated` (`YYYY-MM-DD`);
  - `app` (`markdown|data`, optional; it switches on the SoftwareApplication JSON-LD);
  - `tool` (optional, plan 08): the page gets `tools/<tool>/tool.html` and `<script type="module" src="/tools/<tool>/main.js">` (Task 5). No other page may load a script.
- **Page record** (returned by `build`):
  ```
  { path: '/x/' | '/404.html', url: 'https://tegelapps.com' + path, file: 'x/index.html' | '404.html',
    lang, title, description, updated, alternates: {[lang]: path}, app?: string, tool?: string, article: boolean, indexable: boolean }
  ```
- **`site/build.js` exports:**
  - `loadConfig(path = <site>/config.json) -> Config`
  - `fill(markdown: string, lang: string, config: Config, articles: Page[] = []) -> string`. It throws `Error('placeholder {{…}} unknown or empty')`.
  - `build({ content = <site>/content, out = <site>/public, config = loadConfig() } = {}) -> Page[]`. It empties `out` first. `import.meta.main` runs `build()`.
- **Placeholders, which are the only way content mentions these values:**

  | Placeholder | Renders |
  |---|---|
  | `{{pricing:<app>}}` | the localised price sentence plus the licence sentence |
  | `{{price:<app>}}` | e.g. `$14` |
  | `{{version:<app>}}` | the app's version |
  | `{{download:<app>}}` | the download URL |
  | `{{checkout:<app>}}` | the checkout URL plus `reference_id=site-<slug>` (plan 08 Task 5). Slug: page path without slashes, inner `/` → `-`, root → `home`, so `/ar/markdown/` → `site-ar-markdown`. Set with `url.searchParams.set`, so any query already on the link survives. |
  | `{{email:hello\|support}}` | the address |
  | `{{kvk}}`, `{{vat}}` | the legal ids |
  | `{{business}}`, `{{address}}` | the registered business name and the establishment address (imprint) |
  | `{{articles}}` | the article list |
  | `{{partial:<name>}}` | the contents of `templates/partials/<name>.html` (plan 08's waitlist form) |

- **Layout tokens:**
  - `{{lang}}`, `{{dir}}`, `{{title}}`, `{{description}}`;
  - `{{head}}`: canonical, hreflang, OG/Twitter, JSON-LD and RSS link, generated by `build`;
  - `{{body}}` and `{{updated}}`.
- **`site/config.json`** (the values are spec values; the empty strings are filled in the phase shown):

`{{pricing:<app>}}` renders `(intro ? pricing.intro : pricing.regular)[lang]`, with `{price}` and `{regular}` replaced by `"$" + value`, then a space, then `pricing.licence[lang]`. This puts the licence limits next to every price (rule 6).

`kvk` comes from `.env` `KVK_NUMBER` (the existing registration); `business` (the registered business name) and `address` come from the user's answer (U-6). All three are filled in Task 7, before the first deploy. `vat` is filled only if the tax adviser (H10) confirms a btw-id applies (Task 13).
